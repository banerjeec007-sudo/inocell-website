Who we are

InoCell is an export and trading house built to move advanced nano-biotechnology formulations from lab to field, across borders. We work with an R&D-driven manufacturing partner whose portfolio spans agriculture inputs, animal health and nutrition, and pharma & food additives — and manage everything international buyers need — documentation, quality assurance, and trade execution — to bring those formulations to their market.

We don't manufacture. We connect — sourcing consistently, sourcing responsibly, and handling the trade so our partners can focus on production.
